Dual-Stream Interest & Greedy Collateral Risk Sorting

Every borrow splits into Drawn Debt (base Hub rate from utilization) and Premium Debt (extra interest charged for collateral risk). The Spoke sorts enabled collateral by ascending Collateral Risk (CR_i in 0..1000_00 BPS) and only weights the safest slice needed to cover totalDebt.

  • On-chain refresh rule: RP_u refreshes on risk-increasing actions (borrow, withdraw, setUsingAsCollateral, non-deficit liquidationCall, and explicit updateUserRiskPremium), while supply and repay avoid refreshing gas overhead.
  • Premium Offset trick: At update time, virtual premium shares are calibrated so their asset value equals premium offset; only future index growth above the offset accrues as payable premium.

Per-Position Snapshots via uint32 dynamicConfigKey

In Aave V3, lowering a liquidation threshold immediately impacted every open borrower—creating governance shock liquidations. In Aave V4, updating Collateral Factor (CF), Max Liquidation Bonus (LB), or Liquidation Fee (LF) appends a new version key (up to 2^32 entries per reserve).

Target Health Factor, Dust Guards & Dutch-Auction Bonus

Instead of a fixed 50% close factor, liquidators repay just enough debt to restore the borrower to TargetHealthFactor. A hard-coded DUST_LIQUIDATION_THRESHOLD (1_000 USD) expands allowable repayment so positions cannot get stuck in unliquidatable dust.

  • Share payouts (receiveSharesEnabled): When underlying Hub liquidity is tightly utilized, liquidators can receive Hub supply shares directly instead of underlying tokens.
  • Deficit path: If all collateral is seized and debt remains, debt clears, risk premium resets to 0, and any active Spoke can call Hub.eliminateDeficit using its supplied shares.

Invariant 1 — Drawn Debt Conservation

totalBorrowedShares == sum(spokeDebtShares)

Across every Spoke connected to a Hub asset, aggregate debt shares cannot diverge from individual Spoke debt accounting.

Invariant 2 — Asset Backing Bound

hubAddedAssets >= sum(spokeAddedAssets)

Directional share-to-asset rounding in SharesMath.sol guarantees total Hub assets remain at least the sum of converted Spoke added claims.

Invariant 3 — Supply Shares Conservation

hubAddedShares == sum(spokeAddedShares)

Maintained across add, remove, transferShares, mintFeeShares, and cross-Spoke eliminateDeficit.

Invariant 4 — Index Monotonicity

supplySharePrice & drawnIndex never decrease

Interest accrual, fee minting, repays, and deficit accounting must keep share price and drawn index non-decreasing.
